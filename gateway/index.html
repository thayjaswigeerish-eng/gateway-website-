The Event at a Glance

Three Days of Cinema

Day 1 – Screening

All submitted films are screened before the audience and our panel of expert judges. Short films, documentaries, PSAs, and AI films take the stage.

Day 2 – Discussions

Engage with industry experts in panel discussions. Participate in fun film-themed games, quizzes, and creative workshops.

Day 3 – Prizes

Winners are announced and celebrated. Judges share their remarks and awards are distributed across all film categories.
