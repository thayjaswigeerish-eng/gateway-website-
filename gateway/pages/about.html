About GATEWAY

The Event

What is GATEWAY?

GATEWAY is a national-level film festival conducted by the Department of Media Sciences, College of Engineering Guindy, Anna University, Chennai.

The festival invites filmmakers — students, professionals, and AI enthusiasts — to submit short films, documentaries, public service announcements, and AI-generated short films for screening and evaluation by an expert panel of judges.

It is a platform to celebrate creativity, promote independent cinema, and connect passionate storytellers across the country.

The Experience

3 Days of Cinema & Creativity

Day 1 – Film Screening

All submitted entries are screened in full for the audience and the judging panel. Entries are organised by category — Short Film, Documentary, PSA, and AI Film. The panel evaluates each entry based on creativity, narrative, and technical quality.

Day 2 – Discussions & Games

Day 2 features lively panel discussions with industry professionals on topics like the future of short-form cinema and AI in filmmaking. Participants also take part in film quizzes, games, and hands-on creative activities.

Day 3 – Prize Distribution

The festival concludes with a valedictory ceremony where winners across all categories are felicitated. The judging panel shares remarks and feedback, followed by the prize distribution by the chief guest.
