What You Can Submit

Film Categories

Short Film

Original narrative fiction films. Tell a complete, compelling story in a short runtime. Films will be judged on screenplay, cinematography, direction, and overall impact.

Documentary

Non-fiction films that explore real events, real people, or social issues through authentic storytelling, interviews, and research-backed narrative.

PSA – Public Service Announcement

Short, impactful films crafted to communicate a social message. Judged on clarity of message, emotional impact, and creative execution.

AI-Generated Short Film

Films produced primarily using artificial intelligence tools (image generation, video synthesis, AI voiceover, etc.). Explore the creative frontier of AI cinema and push the boundaries of storytelling.

How We Judge

Judging Criteria

Screenplay

Quality of the story, script structure, originality, and narrative flow.

Cinematography

Visual composition, lighting, camera work, and overall aesthetics.

Editing

Pacing, transitions, sound design, and post-production quality.

Impact

Emotional resonance, message clarity, and overall audience impact.
