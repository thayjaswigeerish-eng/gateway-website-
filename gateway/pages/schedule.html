3-Day Event Programme

Event Schedule

9:00 AM
Inauguration Ceremony

Welcome address by the Head of Department, lamp lighting, and introduction of the chief guest and judging panel.

10:00 AM
Screening – Short Films & PSAs

All submitted short films and public service announcements are screened for the audience and panel judges.

1:00 PM
Lunch Break

Refreshment and networking opportunity for participants and guests.

2:00 PM
Screening – Documentaries & AI Films

Continuation of screenings — documentary and AI-generated film submissions take the stage.

4:30 PM
Audience Q&A with Filmmakers

Participants get a chance to discuss their films with the audience before the panel begins deliberating.

5:30 PM
Day 1 Wrap-Up

Closing remarks for the day. Panel deliberation begins behind closed doors.

9:30 AM
Panel Discussion – The Future of Short Films

Industry experts discuss trends in short-form storytelling, OTT platforms, and the rise of independent cinema in India.

11:00 AM
Panel Discussion – AI in Cinema

A focused conversation on AI-generated films — exploring creativity, ethics, tools, and what lies ahead for filmmakers.

1:00 PM
Lunch Break

Refreshment and networking break.

2:00 PM
Film Quiz & Games

Fun and engaging film-themed quiz, trivia rounds, and creative challenges open to all participants.

3:30 PM
Filmmaking Activities & Mini-Workshops

Hands-on sessions on cinematography basics, editing techniques, and storytelling structure — guided by faculty and industry mentors.

5:00 PM
Day 2 Wrap-Up

Closing remarks for the day and preview of Day 3 prize ceremony.

10:00 AM
Cultural Programme

Opening performances and cultural presentations by Media Sciences students setting the celebratory mood.

11:00 AM
Judges' Remarks & Feedback

The panel judges share their observations, constructive feedback, and highlight standout entries from the festival.

12:00 PM
Prize Distribution Ceremony

Awards are presented across all four film categories — Short Film, Documentary, PSA, and AI-Generated Film. Winners are felicitated by the chief guest.

1:30 PM
Valedictory Function

Closing address by the Head of Department, vote of thanks, and a celebration of all participants and their contributions to GATEWAY 2026.
